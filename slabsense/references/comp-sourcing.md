# SlabSense Comp Sourcing

Use this process whenever a recommendation depends on price, especially for investment intent or listings over $250.

## Required Comp Workflow

1. Start with exact-match sold comps:
   - same card, set, number, language, grading company, and grade
   - sold/completed prices only; keep active asks separate
   - prefer sales from the last 90 days, then widen only if the market is thin
2. Use at least two independent comp sources when available:
   - marketplace page sales history, if visible
   - PriceCharting or other public price aggregators
   - eBay sold listings, 130point, Fanatics, Goldin, Alt, PWCC, or PSA APR when accessible
3. Label every comp by source and confidence:
   - `high`: exact same card/grade with clear sale date and sold price
   - `medium`: exact card/grade from an aggregator where sale details are summarized
   - `low`: nearby grade, stale sale, active ask, best-offer ambiguity, or incomplete title match
4. Reconcile conflicts:
   - If sources disagree by more than 15-20%, say so and lower confidence.
   - Prefer transparent sold rows over aggregate market numbers.
   - Treat outliers as context unless multiple recent sales support them.
5. Only use nearby-grade comps as secondary context.
   - Do not blend PSA 7/9/10 into PSA 8 fair value unless exact-grade comps are unavailable.
   - Clearly mark any grade-adjusted inference.

## Output Requirements

For every SlabSense recommendation, include:

- comp sources checked
- exact comps used with sale date, price, source, and confidence
- active asks or nearby-grade comps in a separate section if used
- a fair-value range derived from exact sold comps when possible
- missing comp coverage when exact sold data is thin or blocked

## Red Flags

Increase regret risk and reduce confidence when:

- there are fewer than three exact sold comps
- comps come from only one marketplace
- source pages are blocked or only visible through screenshots
- sale prices are stale, noisy, or spread more than 25%
- active asks are being used because sold comps are unavailable
- currency, shipping, buyer premium, tax, or import costs materially change the effective price
