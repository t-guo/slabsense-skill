# SlabSense Risk Rubric

Scores use 0-100 where higher liquidity is better, higher regret risk is worse, and higher confidence is better.

## Fair Value

- Use same-card, same-grade sold comps first.
- If 3+ relevant comps exist, fair value is roughly the interquartile middle with light recency judgment.
- If only 1-2 relevant comps exist, widen the range and lower confidence.
- If no relevant comps exist, do not invent value. Use Watch or Pass unless the user explicitly frames it as a non-price personal collection decision.

## Suggested Offer

- Buy candidate: 5-10% below fair value midpoint.
- Watch candidate: 10-20% below fair value midpoint.
- Pass candidate: only provide a conservative "would reconsider near" price if comps support it.

## Liquidity

Increase liquidity when:
- card is a major chase, vintage holo, Charizard/Lugia/Mew/Giratina, Gold Star, Tag Team, or modern alt art
- grade is broadly desirable for the card type
- recent comps are frequent and consistent

Decrease liquidity when:
- niche card, obscure language, weak grade desirability, stale comps, unusual cert/listing concerns
- modern PSA 9 where buyers often prefer raw or PSA 10
- low-grade card with weak eye appeal

## Regret Risk

Increase regret risk for:
- asking price above recent comps
- no clear back photo or poor photo quality
- visible crease, possible surface issue, whitening, or centering concerns
- cert mismatch, missing cert, seller red flags, no returns on high-value purchase
- investment intent with thin liquidity

Decrease regret risk for:
- below-market price with relevant comps
- strong eye appeal for grade
- clear front/back photos
- personal collection intent where flaws are disclosed and priced in

## Verdict

- Buy: price is at or below fair range, confidence is at least moderate, and regret risk is not high.
- Watch: plausible card but missing info, noisy comps, or needs a lower offer.
- Pass: materially overpriced, severe red flags, high regret risk, or insufficient evidence for a high-value buy.
